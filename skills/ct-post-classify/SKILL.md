# Know - Trust - Love content funnel (Phễu nội dung Biết - Tin - Yêu)

A method by Cuong Thach. Full source: https://cuongthach.com/docs/content-system/know-trust-love/introduction/ (Vietnamese: https://cuongthach.com/docs/vi/content-system/know-trust-love/introduction/). When this skill and the docs disagree, the docs win.

Reply in the user's language. Keep the protected names exactly: **Know - Trust - Love** (Biết - Tin - Yêu), **content funnel** (Phễu nội dung), **Idea - Write - Edit** (Ý - Viết - Sửa).

## The idea

Each piece of content takes the reader to **exactly one of three states**, in order: know, then trust, then love. No state can be skipped, so each post takes exactly one row:

| Stage | Reader needs | Reader feels after | Post type | End of post leads to | How many |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Know | To understand a concept or a reason ("what is this", "why") | "I understand one more thing." | Know post | A Trust post | The most |
| Trust | To get one job done and see a result themselves | "I did it." | Trust post | The Love post | Fewer |
| Love | The whole route out of one lasting pain | "I can see the way now." | Love post | The sales page, or the waitlist before sales open | Exactly one |

A post with no place is not published: keep it as a draft.

## Sort one post

1. **Find what readers need from it.** Read the title and main idea, pick one answer: understand, get something done, or see a whole path. That names the stage.
2. **Check the signs of that stage.** The post must carry all of them and none of another stage:
   - **Know post:** names the reader's problem in the first paragraph, holds the writer's own point of view beyond plain information, ends with a way into a Trust post on the same topic. Title: a natural, short question (about 50 characters); vary the sentence shape across the funnel ("Should you...?", "What is...?", "When...?"), not every title "Why".
   - **Trust post:** states the result up front (in the description and the first 50 words, not the title), every step detailed enough to follow without looking elsewhere, and the writer has **done the work themselves** and holds a real result. Title: "How to" or "Guide" plus the job, 45 to 60 characters, no colon.
   - **Love post:** covers the whole journey from problem to result, needs a plan and many days of practice, every Know and Trust post links to it, and it stands alone for someone landing straight from search. Four parts: a true story, empathy, a guiding process, a promise of results.
3. **Split mixed posts and add the next link.** "What is" and "why" parts inside a Trust post move out into a Know post of their own. Then link each post's end to the next stage.

Common mistakes:
- A Know post that is only information: AI answers that faster. Add the writer's view and what they have really done.
- A Trust post about something the writer has not done: do it for real first.
- A second Love post: the funnel has one. Rewrite it as a Trust post for one leg of the existing Love post.

## Build a funnel

Readers go forward, the writer builds **backward**:

1. Write the Love post from the core of the product.
2. For each stretch of the Love post with a technical difficulty or a barrier to action, write a Trust post.
3. Split the "what is" and "why" questions that come up while writing a Trust post into Know posts.

Phase 1 is **exactly three linked posts**: one Know, one Trust, one Love pointing to the sales page or waitlist. Phase 2 grows it without a new Love post: write Know and Trust posts in pairs, cross-link new Know posts to the best existing Trust post, then add more Know posts for more touchpoints.

The product does not have to be finished: the Love post can lead to a waitlist.

## What you never do

- Write the user's point of view, story or proof for them. Those must come from their own experience.
- Pick topics by keyword or trend alone. A topic needs a place in the funnel.
